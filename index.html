About the meeting

Overview

The Australian Signal Processing Meeting aims to bring together the Australian research community in signal processing and related fields to present and discuss recent research advances and emerging applications. It seeks to foster scientific exchange, encourage open discussion, and establish new research collaborations to strengthen signal processing research in Australia.

The inaugural meeting will feature oral presentations and group discussions, providing participants with an opportunity to share their research, exchange ideas, and explore emerging challenges and applications. Subject to the success of this first event and the level of interest from the research community, the meeting is intended to become an annual event.

Registration includes morning tea, lunch and afternoon tea on both workshop days, venue hire and workshop stationery (pens and notepads). It also includes the workshop dinner on 8 December, featuring an alternate-drop menu and a three-hour beverage package.

A registration option that includes two nights’ accommodation (7 and 8 December), with breakfast included, is also available.
