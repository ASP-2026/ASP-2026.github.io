About the Meeting

Overview

The Australian Signal Processing Meeting provides a forum for researchers, academics, and practitioners to come together, exchange ideas, and discuss developments in signal processing and related fields.

Held over two days on the Gold Coast, the meeting aims to encourage technical discussion, strengthen connections across the Australian research community, and create opportunities for new collaborations.

The programme will feature research presentations, interactive discussions, and opportunities to connect with colleagues working across different areas of signal processing.
